# Reference: Carousel Review

> On-demand reference. Read only when reviewing or planning carousels.

## What to capture (raw)
- Number of slides, slide-1 hook text, last-slide CTA.
- Saves and shares vs. the account's single-image and reel averages.
- Topic and whether slides tell one continuous story.

## What to evaluate
- **Hook**: does slide 1 earn the swipe in <8 words?
- **Flow**: one idea per slide, building to a payoff?
- **CTA**: clear next step on the final slide?
- **Save-worthiness**: is it reference material someone would return to?

## Output
```markdown
| Carousel | Slides | Hook | Saves vs avg | Verdict | Fix |
|----------|--------|------|--------------|---------|-----|
| [link] | [n] | [hook] | [+/− %] | [keep/iterate/drop] | [one change] |
```

## Rules
- Compare carousels to the account's own format baselines, not absolutes.
- One concrete fix per carousel. Mark missing numbers `[TO CONFIRM]`.
