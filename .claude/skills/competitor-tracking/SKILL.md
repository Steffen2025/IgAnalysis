# Skill: Competitor Tracking

## Purpose
Identify and track relevant competitor Instagram accounts for a business unit and
summarize what they do that the unit can learn from.

## When to use
When asked to find, vet, or track competitors, or to compare an account against
its competitive set.

## Required inputs
- Business unit and its category (use a clean category, not raw IG strings).
- The account being benchmarked.
- Optional seed competitors or a city/region for local relevance.

## Process
1. Build/confirm the competitor set. Hard-reject irrelevant handles (music,
   tattoo, unrelated niches, junk-category matches).
2. Score relevance by category/keyword overlap; keep a short human reason per pick.
3. Do not analyze every competitor at once — track a focused set.
4. Collect cadence, format mix, and standout posts per competitor (raw data).
5. Summarize patterns and concrete, copyable moves (interpretation), kept separate.

## Output format
Structured markdown: competitor table (handle, why relevant, cadence, formats) +
"What to copy / what to avoid" list. See
`.claude/references/competitor-tracking-reference.md`.

## Quality rules
- Relevance first — reject junk matches and explain why.
- Region-specific when local (Burlington = Burlington, ON).
- Raw data separate from recommendations; mark gaps `[TO CONFIRM]`.
