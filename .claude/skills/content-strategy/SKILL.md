# Skill: Content Strategy

## Purpose
Turn analysis findings into a concrete content plan for a business unit — what to
post, in what format, how often — grounded in the data, not generic advice.

## When to use
After an account/competitor analysis exists and the ask is "what should we do
about it."

## Required inputs
- The analysis/report for the business unit.
- The unit's brand voice (read its brand-voice reference).
- Current cadence and format performance.

## Process
1. Read the unit's brand-voice reference so recommendations sound on-brand.
2. Anchor every recommendation to an observed metric or competitor move.
3. Propose a focused plan: pillars, format mix, cadence, 3–5 next posts.
4. Keep recommendations separate from the underlying raw data.
5. Mark assumptions `[TO CONFIRM]`.

## Output format
Structured markdown: priorities, content pillars, cadence, and a short next-posts
list with hooks. Keep it actionable.

## Quality rules
- Metric-aware and specific — no hype, no generic "post more / be consistent."
- On-brand for the specific business unit.
- Action items first, rationale second.
