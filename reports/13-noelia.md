**Reel 1 — What First-Time Buyers Get Wrong About Pre-Approval**

Hook: Here is what most first-time buyers in Calgary find out too late about pre-approval.

Body:
- A pre-approval letter is not a guarantee — lenders can still pull financing if your situation changes before closing, so avoid new credit applications, job changes, or large purchases after you get it.
- Your pre-approval amount is a ceiling, not a budget. In Calgary's current market, buying at the top of your range leaves no room for property tax increases, condo fees, or unexpected repairs.
- Rate holds vary by lender — some lock your rate for 90 days, some for 120. Ask your mortgage broker the exact date, and align your search timeline to it.

Caption: Pre-approval is step one, but knowing what it actually means is what protects you at the table. If you are buying your first home in Calgary this year, these three things will save you stress and money before you even make an offer. Questions about where to start? Drop them below.

CTA: Save this before your first lender call.

**Reel 2 — What a Calgary Listing Actually Costs a Seller**

Hook: This is the full cost breakdown of selling a home in Calgary that most sellers only see at closing.

Body:
- Real estate commission, legal fees, and potential mortgage payout penalties are the three costs sellers most commonly underestimate — knowing each number in advance lets you price your home with a clear net figure in mind.
- In Calgary, if your home was your principal residence for every year you owned it, you owe no capital gains tax — but if you rented any portion of it, that changes, and a conversation with your accountant before listing is worth having.
- Timing your possession date to align with your next purchase closes the gap where sellers end up paying two mortgages or living out of a hotel.

Caption: Selling your Calgary home without knowing your actual net proceeds is like negotiating blind. Here is the cost picture most people do not see until it is too late to adjust. If you are thinking about listing in the next six months, this is where your planning starts.

CTA: Send this to someone who is thinking about selling.

**Reel 3 — How to Read a Calgary Neighbourhood Before You Buy**

Hook: This is how to evaluate a Calgary neighbourhood before you ever book a showing.

Body:
- Walk or drive the area on a weekday morning and a Friday evening — the same street behaves differently depending on the time, and you will notice things no listing photo will show you.
- Check the community association website for the area. Calgary has active community associations that post rezoning applications, development permits, and neighbourhood concerns publicly — this takes ten minutes and can surface issues that affect your long-term value.
- School boundaries matter even if you do not have children yet, because they affect resale demand. Calgary Board of Education boundary maps are free and searchable by address.

Caption: The right home in the wrong neighbourhood is still the wrong decision. Before you fall in love with a floor plan, do these three checks on the area itself. Calgary's communities each have a distinct character — knowing how to read that quickly will make your search faster and smarter.

CTA: Tag someone currently house-hunting in Calgary.
